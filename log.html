Route notes

The commander started parked in an SRV Scarab on Pleiades Sector LY-Q b5-2 A 2, a body with six Thargoid signals about 340 ly from Sol, and flew home to Diaguandri, 127 ly from Sol. From there Dreaming Void ran north through Sol's neighbourhood to Orrere and back, then 449 ly east in seven jumps to HR 3230, 423 ly from Sol, and back again on Monday. Aurora then ran the three material traders around Diaguandri and went north to two engineers: Selene Jean at Kuk and Hera Tani at Kuwemaki, 264 ly from Sol.

Her four jumps to Kuwemaki averaged 80.5 ly. On Tuesday she came back through Palin, Bill Turner, Didi Vatermann, Lei Cheung, The Dweller and Ram Tah, and home to Diaguandri. On Thursday she took Aurora to Achenar to unlock Tiana Fortune, back to Diaguandri for her stored power plant and the brokers, then to Hera Tani at Kuwemaki and Bill Turner at Alioth.

On Friday Aurora flew from Alioth to Ram Tah at Meene and home, and Void Runner went south to Damna for rare goods, sold them at Starport Of Starlight in Arietis Sector RY-R b4-4 and came home. On Saturday Artemis hunted bounties at the Diaguandri nav beacon and Void Runner worked signal sources in Diaguandri, with one 11 ly hop to Arietis Sector UJ-Q b5-1 and back. On Sunday Void Runner carried Li Yong-Rui's commodities around the Arietis sectors next door, and Aurora then ran north-east through 76 Sigma Ceti to Yoru and Caledo, 230 ly from home, on two courier jobs.

On Monday she worked the systems around Yoru in short hops for the Party of Yoru and flew Aurora home; Void Runner then went north to Xihe for Zacariah Nemo's price, on to Hollos and Yoru, and home again. On Tuesday Aurora went to Professor Palin at Arque for an anti-corrosion cargo rack, and Invictus carried it south to HIP 20567 for Meta-Alloys, then north past Sol to Epsilon Indi, EZ Aquarii and Altair. On Wednesday Invictus sold her hold at Luyten's Star and came home, and Aurora went back east to HR 3230 by way of LHS 417.

On Thursday Aurora flew home and ran the three brokers, and Invictus fetched palladium from LHS 501. Her last jump, 56.5 ly, was back to Diaguandri from the detainment centre in Tascheter Sector AV-Y b5, which she reached without a jump and which is not plotted. On Friday the new Caspian flew her first engineering circuit, Farseer at Deciat and Lei Cheung at Laksak, then a second to Ram Tah and back to Laksak; Aurora went out to a crash site at Koli Discii and came home by the raw trader.

On Saturday the Caspian, named Hymn of the Long Night that evening, flew two more circuits: Palin, the Dweller, Selene Jean and Bill Turner, then Hera Tani at Kuwemaki twice. That evening her shakedown cruise took her to the neutron star Jackson's Lighthouse, whose jet cone boosted a 207.7 ly jump back to HIP 15609, the longest in the record, and on to Lei Cheung and home. On Sunday she left for the expedition proper, five jumps south-west through Arietis and Col 285 Sectors to Synuefai, about 510 ly from Sol, and spent the day hopping between a dozen systems there and in Mel 22 Sector to sample what grew on them.

On Monday she pushed on south-west to HIP 16403 and Wredguia EM-E b25-1, about 605 ly from Sol. On Tuesday she went on through Synuefai, five long jumps south-west to Synuefai SL-Q b11-2, about 970 ly from Sol. On Wednesday she pressed on past 1,100 ly from Sol to Synuefai SE-C c2-4, and on Thursday worked three more Synuefai systems nearby.

On Friday she went on into Oochost, charged her drive at the neutron star Oochost JE-E d13-16 for a 393.5 ly jump east, the longest in the record, reached about 1,400 ly from Sol at Oochost TK-D b46-0, and turned north into Synuefai again. On Saturday she kept north through Synuefai, past a T Tauri star, to Mel 22 Sector and the edge of the Taurus Dark Region, back to about 555 ly from Sol. On Sunday the last samples took her through the Taurus Dark Region to the Pleiades, about 430 ly from Sol, where the expedition ended with a four-jump course plotted for Diaguandri.

She flew it on Monday morning, after a Thargoid hyperdiction pulled her out of the first jump, sold the expedition's data at Ray Gateway, and went on in two jumps to Sol. That afternoon she set out for Colonia by the scenic route: 59 jumps, 31 of them supercharged at neutron stars, past the Red Spider, Omega, Lagoon, Trifid and Eagle nebulae to Skaudai YP-O e6-17, about 11,780 ly from Sol. On Tuesday 35 more jumps, 26 of them boosted, took her past the Fields of Elysium, Arcane Allure, Matryoshka, Evelyn's Light and Monde de la Mort to Colonia, 22,000 ly from Sol.

On Wednesday she began to learn her way around it: Kinesi and Carcosa, a few dozen light years away. On Thursday she flew back to Diaguandri in a single run of 84 jumps, then took Invictus to LFT 509, a system the Pilots Federation had just opened. That evening Invictus flew a Powerplay round north through Arietis and Col 285 and home.

On Friday Invictus worked power wreckage in Simarasir and Volkhabe, Void Runner carried Altairian Skin from Altair to Volkhabe, and in the evening Hymn of the Long Night set out for Colonia again: 36 jumps, 14 of them boosted, coreward and below the galactic plane to Pyraleau HB-H b39-0, about 6,870 ly from Sol. On Saturday she crossed the rest of the way in 61 jumps, 30 of them boosted, past Boeph to Colonia, 22,000 ly from Sol. On Sunday Hephaestus worked within 30 ly of Colonia, to Tir, Luchtaine, Magellan and Los and back, and Hymn of the Long Night went to Carcosa and back, then to Ogmar, 7 ly from Colonia, and once to Magellan and back for the settlement jobs.

On Monday Hymn looped from Ogmar through Asura and Santos Dumont, then flew by Colonia to Carcosa and back to Jaques Station. On Tuesday Artemis flew from Colonia to Ogmar, Kinesi and back, then to Ratraii and Luchtaine. On Wednesday Artemis went to Tenjin and back to Colonia, and Hymn of the Long Night on to Carcosa.

On Thursday Hymn flew home from Carcosa to Diaguandri in a single run of 85 jumps, then on to LHS 1163. On Friday she crossed to Fiden, by way of Teleu, for a raid at Makonda Leisure Complex.
