Entry 13308The voice in the cockpit

For nine years my job was to make ships talk. I was on the cockpit voice team at Gutamaya, in Achenar, and if you've ever heard a Clipper tell you the frame shift drive is charging, there's a fair chance I argued with someone about how long the pause before "charging" should be.

I liked the work, I really did, right up until I worked out I'd spent most of a decade writing the calm voice that tells a pilot their hull is failing and I had never once been the pilot, and after that I couldn't stop thinking about it. So in the summer of 3308 (2022) I handed in my notice and bought the cheapest Hauler I could find. I named her Horizon. I've never decided whether that was hopeful or a joke, and I still own her, so I suppose it doesn't matter.

The engineers found me faster than I expected. Or I found them, depends who you ask. Farseer, Martuuk, the Dweller, McQuinn, Ryder, all on rumour and other pilots' bad directions. I went through three Asp Explorers in quick succession, which was mostly a way of learning what I didn't want, and then in August I sat in a Diamondback Explorer and stopped looking. Dreaming Void. She's still the ship my registration lists.

I flew Federation contracts for a while without thinking much about whose flag was on them, and they made me an Ensign for it. Farseer did my drive. In September I bought a Gunship.

I'll write about what came after that some other time.

Entry 23310Coming home in Imperial colours

August 3310 (2024).

I came back wearing purple which surprised a few people who'd known me as the woman flying Federation contracts, it surprised me less. I bought a pair of Type-8s, kept the better one and named her Compass Rose, and the Navy moved me from Outsider to Baron so quickly that I can only assume somebody had been doing my paperwork while I was away.

Later that month I bought a Clipper. Void Runner. She's a Gutamaya hull, and the first thing I did after the docking clamps released was pull up her voice build to see whether any of my old code was still in there. Some of it is. I still check every time I take her out and yes, I know that's a bit sad.

September was expensive. A Krait Phantom I named Invictus, a Fer-de-Lance I named Artemis, and a Krait Mk II that I flew briefly and sold, because some ships just don't sit right. The Navy made me a Viscount. I pledged to Li Yong-Rui, mostly because his systems were where the work was.

That same month I took Invictus into the Pleiades and met the Thargoids. I'm not going to put any more about that in the log.

The Scarab was exactly where I'd left her, parked on a grey moon with six Thargoid sites over the horizon and Diaguandri about 220 light years behind me. She didn't want to start. I killed the power on the first attempt because something sounded wrong, then had a panel open twice for two different faults, each time shutting down and bringing her back up to see if the fix had held. The fourth start was clean.

After that I just sat. Connected to the network, didn't touch the throttle, and caught up. It came to seventy-seven minutes, according to the log. I had two years of charts in the data banks that nobody had paid me for so I worked out the shortest way back to Diaguandri and what Ray Gateway was paying for data now, and then, because I've had an idea about the Mandalay for a while, I went looking for an outfitter near home that stocks field maintenance units. I read up on the powers, too, since a lot has changed, and on what Li Yong-Rui does for pledged pilots these days.

The tea was cold by the time I'd finished. Shut down at 01:47. Next time I power up, I'm going home.

Entry 4Oct 3312Twenty-six jumps, cut to ten
systems up 14:50 GST

About a minute and a half after I brought the Scarab up, every system in her died at once. Six Thargoid signals on this moon, so I'm calling it a shutdown pulse, and I didn't hang around to find out whether there'd be a second. I drove her into Void Runner's hangar, lifted off and had the Clipper in supercruise before I'd properly finished being frightened. I did that later, in supercruise, with the tea.

The plotter offered me twenty-six jumps home. I don't know who writes those defaults (not me, for once) but I got it down to ten and scooped at every star through the Col 285 and Arietis sectors. At Bharani a Viper pilot locked me four times in about three seconds, thought it over, and let me go. I'd have done the same in their place. A Clipper is a lot of ship to argue with.

The honk at Diaguandri came back with 146 signals, and 115 of them were fleet carriers. I don't remember the neighbourhood being this crowded.

Ray Gateway paid a bit under 1.9 million for two years of charts. Not one first discovery in the whole lot. Which tells you how thoroughly the Pleiades have been walked over. A crate of Meta-Alloys I'd forgotten about went for 214,000. The Neofabric sold for slightly less than I'd paid. I decided I could live with that.

The Lynx Highliner I'd ordered before I left was still waiting at the yard. She's going to be Helios, the rescue ship I've been meaning to build for years.

Entry 5Oct 3312Athena becomes Aurora
systems up 17:22 GST

I walked past Void Runner and the new Lynx without stopping and took the Mandalay out instead. One jump to Ebor, where a Stronghold Carrier was sitting above the fourth planet, and it turned me away at the door: restricted access.

So I renewed my pledge to Li Yong-Rui right there, sitting in front of it, two years after the first time, I don't think I even finished reading the terms. I'd like to say the carrier was impressed. It refused me twice more, once because I was too far away and once because it had no free pad, and then it scanned my cargo on the way out, presumably on principle. I didn't dock.

Caillie Town, then. 10th Hawk Squadron run it, and they're in the middle of an expansion, so the place was busy and the outfitters were cheerful. I spent close to six million credits. The mining lasers came out, and two field maintenance units went in, a big one and a small one, along with a wake scanner, point defence and a better docking computer. I also swapped the 3A power plant for a lighter 2A, which was a mistake, although I didn't know that yet.

She doesn't mine any more, so Athena didn't fit her. She's Aurora now, RI-X04, and she's an explorer.

The 2A can't carry everything I put in. The Guardian booster went dark after the refit and I didn't notice, which cost me ten and a half light years of range I didn't know I'd lost. Either Hera Tani overcharges the plant or the old 3A comes out of storage. Until one of those happens, she stays in the hangar.

When it finally came up, I mapped Orrere 2 b with two probes, which is as clean as the scan gets, set down and took the Scarab out. Some gold, some beryllium.

Shut down in the buggy. Dreaming Void's landing lights were still on, up on the ridge where I'd left her, and I watched them for a while before I turned everything off.

I'm somewhere under 1,700 light years short of my hundred-thousandth in hyperspace. I didn't think I'd care about a number like that, but apparently I do.

Entry 8Oct 3312Brokers, a bad trade, and two engineers
systems up 23:28 GSTHR 3230 3 a a

Seven jumps home in Dreaming Void, the same empty stars in the opposite order. Coming into Ray Gateway I scanned whatever was in range out of habit, and Li Yong-Rui's people paid me eight merits a time for it. Then they paid me two thousand all at once and didn't say why. Then I swapped to Aurora and got on with the real job. I'd made a list of everything her engineers would want, and there are three material brokers within a jump of home. The encoded broker at Ray Gateway was easy. At Thomas Orbital in Wolf 1241 I made a mess of it: I traded 144 iron for four tin, then traded six tin back for one selenium, and somehow left with less tin than I'd arrived with. I've written trading logic. I have no idea how I managed that, I genuinely don't. Tesla Terminal in Njulngan was nine trades without a single error, so I suppose it evened out. I went back to Wolf 1241 afterwards and sorted the tin out with mercury, molybdenum and niobium I'd had no use for anyway.

Then north, and for the first time Aurora jumped the way I'd built her to. Eighty light years, then sixty-nine, to Kuk, where Selene Jean reinforced the hull plating at Prospector's Rest over sixteen passes. Four more long jumps and a short hop took me to Kuwemaki. Hera Tani overcharged the power plant at The Jet's Hole and it came out at 13.44 megawatts, which is as high as that process will go. I was pleased about that for roughly a minute until I did the sums and worked out that with both maintenance units off she still draws five percent more than the plant gives her, so the wake scanner will have to go.

Docked at The Jet's Hole, shut down 01:13 GST, 259 light years from Diaguandri. She's about half done.

I'd been one step short of his full trust for longer than I like to think about, and he gave it to me while he was halfway through the heat sink launcher, without making any fuss about it. Home to Ray Gateway after that. I sold a few charts and picked up four more merits, and shut down at 00:44 GST. My hands ached from tapping on the console the whole way round. Nine of her systems are done now, and two shield boosters I still haven't fitted are the only thing left.

Entry 10Oct 3312As finished as she gets, for now
Ray Gateway, Diaguandri

I've decided Aurora is done, or as done as I can make her from here.

Some of it is still loose. The wake scanner was never engineered. Ram Tah didn't quite finish the point defence turret, and I didn't go back to make him. The two extra shield boosters I'd planned never went in at all; she flies with the scanner and the turret in those slots instead, and having flown her like that, I think I prefer it.

The life support is the part that keeps bothering me. Bill Turner did a good job and it's the best anyone in the bubble can do. Etienne Dorn could do better, and Dorn is in Colonia, twenty-two thousand light years from this dock. So are Mel Brandon, Petra Olmanova and Marsha Hicks. I've been hearing about all four of them for years and I've never met any of them.

I've been flying since 3308 (2022), I've crossed a hundred thousand light years in hyperspace, and I have never made the run to Colonia. There's always been a reason. A contract, a refit, the two years I don't write about. I didn't expect it to bother me this much, it does though. I have a ship that can cross ninety light years in a jump, and none of the best workshops in that part of the galaxy have ever seen her.

I'll go one day. I keep saying that.

Entry 11Oct 3312Plans for a seeker
Ray Gateway, Diaguandri

I should have written about this sooner.

All the way round Aurora's engineers I had time on my hands, more than I'd expected. Hyperspace tunnels, long supercruise approaches, sitting on landing pads while someone else took my ship apart. I used most of it to design the next one.

She's a Caspian Explorer, built for exobiology. I've been calling her Reliant Seeker, though I'm not sure the name will survive the shipyard. Aurora is built to go a long way and keep herself in one piece out there. Reliant Seeker is meant to land and let me go outside and find out what's growing on the worlds Aurora flies past.

The build is done down to the last module. She has a fuel scoop, a Guardian booster, a surface scanner and a field maintenance unit for the long runs. There's a large vehicle hangar for a Nomad, so I can fly down between colonies instead of walking. On paper she'll jump close to eighty-eight light years. I've even planned out which engineer gets which module and the route between them, with Farseer first so that every jump after that one is on the finished drive. Some of that I worked out in Lei Cheung's waiting area with my tea going cold beside me.

What I don't have is the ship. Or the money for her, or the materials for that matter. Aurora's refit went through a lot of the stock, and the list of what I'm short of has grown since I first wrote it down. So it's back to selling charts and working the brokers for a while, and Reliant Seeker stays in the planning files until I can afford to build her.

Entry 16Oct 3312Wrapping up loose ends
01:40 GST, systems downRay Gateway, Diaguandri

Right, so I've decided I'm going to Colonia. There are some engineers out there I need to make contact with, and Aurora needs a shakedown cruise anyway, so I think it'll fit just fine.

That being said there are still a couple of things I need to take care of here at home first. Li Yong-Rui has requests that need finishing, and that means knocking the mothballs off Artemis, unfortunately. There are bounties that need collecting, and while I'm not a fan of shooting down other ships, sometimes needs must.

And after that? It's been four years since I left Gutamaya and I think I'm going to rewrite the COVAS in my ships. I'm tired of the standard software, so I'm going to do what the company never let me do and push it as close to illegal as I can without crossing that line.

First, I'm redoing the visual HUD. I'm tired of that orange, it's hard on the eyes, especially when you're out in the black for as long as I'm going to be.

Then the voice. Smooth it out, make it less robotic, give it a flawless aristocratic Imperial accent. Something that works for me.

Then the dynamic prioritisation matrix. I'm going to link it to my biometric data so it suppresses the low-priority announcements during high-stress situations.

Then I'm piping the raw data stream from the passive emissive sensors straight into the voice engine. Instead of it just saying "warning, thermal attack," it'll tell me what type of attack it is and where it's coming from.

Then I'm making it context-aware: have it react to my commands. It won't be an AI (I won't cross those legal boundaries), it'll just act on combinations of words, voice-triggered macros that know what's going on around them. If I yell "get us out of here," the system is going to drop a heat sink, shift everything into engines and get me into supercruise as fast as possible.

Then I'm adding a scraper to bridge the ship's local frame buffer with the live galactic mapping tools, so it tells me which system I'm entering, the pirate activity and what the power struggle looks like there.

Finally, I'm setting up random phrases that play on distinct events. Give the ship a little attitude, shall we say. If the shields go offline it'll give me a line about it. I should be able to find some suitable phrases on GalNet.

Entry 22Oct 3312Expensive metal
