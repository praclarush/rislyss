Chapter five · October 3312

Sol and the road to Colonia

With the expedition's data sold, she stayed at Ray Gateway only long enough for the ship to be serviced, then set out for Colonia by way of Sol, which she had passed many times and never stopped to see.

Oct 331214:45 to 15:42Sol

The cradle

Two jumps took Hymn of the Long Night to Sol. She scanned the system's nav beacon, then dropped to Earth for its memorials, the listening posts of the Memorial to the Second Thargoid War and the Pilots' Memorial, and the tourist beacon called the Cradle of Humanity.

At Mars she scanned the beacon over the Nonhuman Relic. She then turned outward and spent a long stretch in supercruise to reach one of the ancient uncrewed probes launched from Earth before humanity left it, far beyond the planets, where she shut down at 15:42.

Oct 331218:42 to 01:48Sol to Skaudai

Into the nebulae

She entered the course for Colonia into the navigation computer at Sol and left by the sightseeing route: Rhapsody in Blue at HIP 111647, then the first neutron stars of the journey and the IC 1287 Nebula. In the jet cone of the neutron star at Bleia Eohn RY-Y d1-24 the ship was caught and could not be pulled clear; she rode the stream to its end before the drive took the charge and the jump completed.

In Red Spider Sector KI-S b4-1, beside the Red Spider Nebula, she found two notable stellar phenomena: a Roseum Lagrange cloud holding Purpureum Metallic Crystals, and a Viride cloud holding Rubeum Metallic Crystals. Supercharged jumps took her on through the Omega, Lagoon and Trifid nebulae to the Eagle Nebula, where she stopped at the centre of the sector beside the Pillars of Creation.

Beyond the Eagle she passed Viridian Dreams, a green gas giant at Nyeajaae IT-M c9-19, and the Collection of Wonders at Skaude AA-A h294, a system with two black holes and three ringed stars. At Skaudai YP-O e6-17, Campana Aurora, three more phenomena: a Luteolum cloud with Flavum and Purpureum crystals, a Croceum cloud with Solid Mineral Spheres and Rubeum crystals, and a Viride cloud with Rubeum crystals and Lattice Mineral Spheres.

Fifty-nine jumps, thirty-one of them supercharged, carried her about 11,780 light years from Sol, more than half the way to Colonia. She shut down at Skaudai at 01:48.

Oct 331214:16 to 01:51Skaudai to Colonia

Colonia

Past the Skaudai Guardian ruins system she came to the Fields of Elysium at Clooku GR-U d3-112: two Earth-like worlds, both ringed, in close orbit about each other. She stayed to look. Beyond them Arcane Allure, at Boelts QJ-Y d1-1149, a ringed green gas giant with water-based life, and Matryoshka at Eoch Flyuae YK-K c10-56, three bodies whose orbits appear to carry them through one another. Universal Cartographics' explanation is that the three share a single orbital track in a slow resonance, and from most vantage points pass so close that they seem to overlap. At close range a gap always remains.

At Evelyn's Light, the memorial beacon to Evelyn Faye Roy, she stopped and scanned the listening post. On the way she made the longest jump in her record, a supercharged 501.7 light years. Monde de la Mort followed, and a last sampling stop in Spoihaae RD-Z d1-224, where Tussock Catena and Frutexa Flammasis, both new to her Codex, grew on two moons.

She reached Colonia and docked at Jaques Station, 22,000 light years from Sol, after 94 jumps from Sol, 57 of them supercharged. The frame shift drive had worn to 96.6 percent, far less than she had expected; the yard restored it. Vista Genomics paid 353,830,400 credits for six analyses, every one of them first logged by her, and Universal Cartographics 18,515,907 for the systems behind her. She arranged for Hephaestus and Artemis to be brought out from Diaguandri, at a cost of 221,272,144 credits. She shut down at 01:51.
