Chapter six · October 3312

Colonia

Having arrived, she set about learning the region she had crossed the galaxy to reach.

Oct 331220:07 to 00:08Colonia, Kinesi, Carcosa

Settling in

She visited five settlements on two of Colonia's moons, going ashore briefly at each: Shore Cultivation Collection and Fiorentino Hydroponics Estate on Colonia 2 a, and Lavoie Genetics Institution, Hahn Hydroponics Collection and Romano Drilling Facility on Colonia 3 c a. At Jaques Station she had a large missile rack fitted to Hymn of the Long Night, the ship's first weapon, which brought her range to 90.8 light years.

She then flew to Kinesi, docking at Mortimer's Charm, and to Carcosa, where Robardin Rock is held by The Nameless, a faction at war. Near Carvalho Mineralogic Facility on Carcosa AB 1 h she came under fire. The megaship Dove Enigma had no berth for her, and she returned to Jaques Station, took a courier contract for the Colonia Council and delivered it to Robardin Rock. She shut down there at 00:08.

Oct 331213:17 to 15:38Colonia to Diaguandri

The way home

A notice from the Pilots Federation that it would issue permits for LFT 509, a system closed until then, brought her back to the bubble. She left Jaques Station and flew to Diaguandri in a single run of 84 jumps, 46 of them supercharged at neutron stars, covering 23,111 light years. Four of them passed 503 light years, the longest she has made. She docked at Ray Gateway two hours and ten minutes after leaving Jaques Station. Hephaestus and Artemis, sent for from Colonia, were still in transit there.
